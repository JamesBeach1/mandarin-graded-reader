字典 · Dictionary

啡
fēi · ㄈㄟ
Used in 咖啡 (kāfēi) — coffee. A loan character: the mouth radical 口 signals pronunciation, borrowed purely for its sound.

⚠ Homophone check: 非 fēi (“not”) sounds identical — 咖啡 is the only common word using 啡.

字源 · Etymology

六书 · 假借 (phonetic loan)口 · mouth radical
啡 carries no original meaning of its own. When coffee arrived in China in the late 1800s, writers needed a sound for the second syllable of “coffee”, so they borrowed 啡 purely for its pronunciation (fēi). The 口 mouth radical hints it belongs to speech/sound.

Radical decomposition: 口 (mouth) + 非 fēi (phonetic component, “not”).

Mnemonic: “Coffee is not (非) something you say (口) — it's something you drink.”

您 vs 你 — the polite you

The waiter addresses you as 您 (nín), the polite form of 你. Service workers, elders and strangers default to 您; using it signals respect and a little social distance. Replying to a barista, plain 你 is perfectly natural.

Tip: when the story's difficulty slider drops to HSK 1, 您 is often replaced by 你 — watch for it in the grammar overlay.
